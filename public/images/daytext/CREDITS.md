# Daily verse photographs

Background photos for the "Tekst van de dag" card and its share image: one for
every day of the year, so a date never shares its photo with another date in
the same year.
All from Pexels, under the Pexels License (https://www.pexels.com/license/):
free for commercial use, no attribution required. Credited here anyway.
Each file is a centre crop to 3:4, resized to 900x1200, JPEG q65, metadata stripped.

Every photo is calm, mid-bright and almost free of fine detail, so white text
stays readable and nothing competes with the verse. 101.jpg onwards were picked
against bands measured off 001.jpg .. 100.jpg: mean luminance 0.33 - 0.66,
top-left quadrant at most 0.82 (where the text sits), contrast at most 0.21,
edge energy at most 0.095 and saturation at most 0.50, no people, buildings,
roads or animals, and at most four photos by one photographer.
